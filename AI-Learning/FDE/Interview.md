Companies might split FDE roles differently and as such the FDE interviews change accordingly to the company. 

### Decomposition interview

| Step                                | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Competency                                                              | Skills                                          |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| **Problem understanding**           | Given a vague prompt and you must identify and ask relevant questions to identify:<br>- [[Stakeholders]] <br>- **Resources:** resources you might use to solve the pain points of the stakeholders<br>- **Timeline**: how much time you have to build the product given the **limited resources** | [[product-sense]]<br>                                                   | [[stakeholders understanding]]<br>[[ambiguity]] |
| **[[stakeholders]] deep dive**      | - Understand the **main pain points** of the stakeholders, not the symptoms that customers usually describe but the cause<br><br>- At the end you should have a well-defined specific definition of the problem you are going to solve                                                            | [[product-sense]]                                                       | [[stakeholders understanding]]                  |
| **Solution Brainstorming**          | - brainstorm potential solutions to the pain points of the [[stakeholders]] given the resources you have.<br>- ask any extra question that might lead you to a better solution. **Ask, don't assume.**                                                                                            | [[product-sense]]                                                       | [[stakeholders understanding]]                  |
| **Scope First Deliverable**         | - what to build and what **not** to build?<br>- Define what are must have and what are nice to have, given the **limited** resources of this first deliverable. Next iterations are about building on top of the previous iteration.                                                              | [[software-engineering]]                                                |                                                 |
| **Design High Level System Design** | - Functional requirements (max 2 or 3)<br>- Non functional requirements (max 2)<br>- General architecture<br>- Deep dive into one area                                                                                                                                                            | [[software-engineering]]                                                | [[system-design]]                               |
| **Customer Handoff and Iteration**  | - Prepare the customer deliverable. For example: <br>   1. Solution in customer language (talking only vs slides)<br>   2. Trade-offs you considered<br>   3. Solution demo<br>   4. Next steps                                                                                                   | [[executive-presence]]<br>[[product-sense]]<br>[[software-engineering]] |                                                 |
