Companies might split FDE roles differently and as such the FDE interviews change accordingly to the company.

The typical FDE interview is composed of:

### Decomposition question: 
1. **Problem understanding**: You're given a vague prompt and you must identify and ask relevant questions to identify
	- [[Stakeholders]] 
	- Resources: resources you might use to solve the pain points of the stakeholders
	- Timeline: how much time you have to build the product given the **limited resources**
2.  [[stakeholders]] deep dive
3. Brainstorming solution(s) to fix the pain points of the [[stakeholders]]
4. Scoping an first deliverable: from a technical side, what can you build? Define what are must have and what are nice to have, given the limited resources of this first deliverable. Next iterations are about building on top of the previous iteration
5. [[system-design]]: a high level system design question **scoped** for the deliverable you defined in the step before.
