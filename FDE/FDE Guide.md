
**What kind of companies hire FDE?**
Companies that have a technical product and customers who are not technical.

For examples, Claude has Claude Code, Claude Cowork, mcps, tools, workflows. One of their customers profiles are F500s in healthcare, finance and defense. 

**What does an FDE do?**
FDE build solutions for specific customers using the company products. Including:  Discovery -> Framing -> Building code in prod -> Monitoring. 

**What is the function of an FDE must have?**
FDEs play multiple hats:
- **Consultant:** gain the trust of the customer (e.g: Executive sponsor, VP, CEO)
- **Product Manager:** product sense. What to build (and what not to build) given limited resources (mainly eng resources and time)
- **Software Engineer:**  building and monitoring the product

**Values**
- **Ownership**: ownership of the product you are delivering to a specific customer
