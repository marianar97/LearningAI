
**What kind of companies hire FDE?**
Companies that have a technical product and a customer who is not technical.

For examples, Claude has claude code, claude co-work, mcps, tools, workflows. One of their customers profiles are F500s in healthcare, finance and defense. 

**What does an FDE do?**
FDE build solutions for specific customers using the company products. Including: 
Discovery -> Framing -> Building code in prod -> Monitoring.
